Hill Rush - physics hill climb driving game (Windows)
======================================================

Just double-click HillRush.exe. Nothing to install.
(If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, click "More info" ->
"Run anyway" - the game isn't code-signed.)

Controls
    Right / D ......... gas          Left / A ...... brake / reverse
    Esc / P ........... pause        R ............. restart
    F11 ............... fullscreen   F12 ........... screenshot
    In the air: Right tips the car back, Left tips it forward.
    Gamepads work too, and you can click the pedals on screen.

Online races with friends
    Main menu -> ONLINE RACE. One player presses HOST A RACE; the others
    see the room in their list (same Wi-Fi / network) and press JOIN, or
    type the address shown in the host's room under "Join by address".
    The first time you host, Windows Firewall asks whether Hill Rush may
    use the network: tick "Private networks" and click "Allow access".
    The game uses UDP ports 47820 and 47821. Everyone needs the same
    version of Hill Rush; Windows and Linux players can race each other.

Your progress is saved in %APPDATA%\HillRush\save.txt
(paste %APPDATA%\HillRush into the Explorer address bar to open it).

Requirements: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 with OpenGL 3.3 graphics drivers.
